## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

An installation administrator can edit its name, logo, icon, approved links and supported visibility rules, preview them, and save a revision that other users see after reload. Cancel leaves the existing identity unchanged; bad files, disconnections and competing edits explain what happened and offer a safe next action without erasing the draft. Ordinary members cannot change identity even by sending direct requests, and personal appearance stays separate.
