> **Medium Risk**
> Changes APNS payload shape and iOS extension rendering for multiple notification types; regressions would affect lock-screen grouping and avatars but not auth or data integrity.
>
> **Overview**
> **Improves iOS push notifications** by driving the communication-notification layout from richer APNS custom data and bundled fallback avatars when sender photos are missing.
>
> **APNS payload & metadata:** `PushNotificationData` gains `notificationType`, `communicationTitle`, `groupName`, and `conversationId`. Channel sends/mentions/replies populate a channel-less sender line and `#channel` group line while keeping legacy alert titles unchanged. `new_email` always sets `mutable-content`, threads by `conversationId`, and can carry `senderPhotoUrl` (resolved in email sync from Macro profile or contact photo). OpenAPI/SDK/generated clients and GraphQL `senderPhotoUrl` follow the same schema.
>
> **iOS Notification Service Extension:** Reads the new `userInfo` fields, builds `INSendMessageIntent` with group recipients when needed, and uses generic **email** or **channel** PNGs when there is no downloaded profile image. Extension resources and entitlements are wired in the Xcode project.
>
> **Tests** cover APNS field wiring for channel/email/call-started cases.
