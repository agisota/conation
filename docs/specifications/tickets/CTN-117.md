> [!NOTE]
> **High Risk**
> Touches authentication boundaries, durable-object persistence, and irreversible migration/seal semantics where bugs could leak access, split-brain writes, or lose collaborative state.
>
> **Overview**
> Introduces **isolated collaborative surfaces** alongside legacy document sync: same UUID can back a document session and a `surface:<uuid>` session with separate storage, grants, and snapshots. Legacy document JWTs stay on [`encode`]/[`decode`]; surfaces get dedicated claims and [`encode_surface`]/[`decode_surface`] with explicit `session_kind`, strict expiry, and no cross-kind authorization.
>
> The **sync service** routes `/surface/{id}/*`, enforces surface lifecycle (pending → ready → revoked), stricter write rules (Edit+ on surfaces vs Comment on documents), socket grant revalidation, and blocks document APIs on surface keys. **Document → surface migration** adds internal freeze/thaw/seal/retire on the legacy DO and import/verify/activate on the target, with SHA-256 snapshot proofs and worker-orchestrated activation that seals the source before enabling the target.
>
> **`sync_service_client`** exposes typed HTTP helpers for that lifecycle. Extensive Miniflare tests cover isolation, revocation/expiry, and migration safety (including optional fault-injection builds).
>
