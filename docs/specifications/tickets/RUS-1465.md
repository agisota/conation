## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Authorized people can upload and read attachments and keep a reusable reference for later forwarding without waiting for mail sending. Personal files spend personal allowance, team files spend team allowance, and failed uploads spend neither. Previously issued links stop working after revocation, while policy-approved large files are processed rather than silently skipped.
