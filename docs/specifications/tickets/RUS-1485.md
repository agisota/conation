## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Only current room members can enter, renew access, or read call recordings and transcripts, including through the assistant; fake or replayed notifications cannot change a call. Each person can grant or withdraw recording and transcription separately, see the real status and stop either operation without ending an otherwise safe unrecorded conversation. Long calls recover from expiring credentials, and optional noise filtering cannot break audio. Immediate loss of access through an already issued artifact link remains blocked until a real revocation mechanism is proven.
