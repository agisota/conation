## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

People can browse meetings they are allowed to see, including those without a recording, and filter by date, source and participant. They can search real recorded speech and summaries, open only permitted results and see an honest “no recording” status elsewhere. Withdrawn or expired material disappears from the feed and search and cannot be reopened from an old link; removing a calendar event does not erase a surviving recording.
