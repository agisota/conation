## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Agents use only information, channels and tools their owner has explicitly allowed; disabled connections cannot be called through a direct service request. Before an agent sends, invites, deletes, publishes or enables a schedule, the owner sees the source and exact proposed effect and can allow or decline it. Rejected, expired, canceled, repeated or altered proposals do nothing, even when an email or document instructs the agent to bypass the rules.
