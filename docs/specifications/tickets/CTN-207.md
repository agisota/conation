> **Overview**
> Adds **standalone meetings and external guests** to the calls stack, with a large **sqlx offline query cache** refresh (mostly from new/updated integration tests).
>
> **Calls & meetings:** Active calls load `meeting_id` and resolve display names from `call_meetings.title` instead of ad-hoc `custom_name` on live rows. >
> **Guests:** Guest participants are tracked in `call_guests`, included in “anyone still in the call?” checks and participant counts, and **copied into `call_record_guests`** when a session is archived.
>
> **Visibility:** Call-record listing/filtering is tightened so channel-less recordings rely on direct user `entity_access`, and link/team share paths apply only when a `channel_id` is present.
>
> The diff also removes/replaces a few heavy list queries and adds many cached queries for unrelated domains (calendar, email, permissions) used by expanded tests.
