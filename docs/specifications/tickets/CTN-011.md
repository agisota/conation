## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

People can open an event's physical location in a map without copying its address. When someone shares a link to an X or Discord conversation, or a map, in a discussion or document, readers can recognize where it leads and open the original conversation or place from either a computer or phone. The interface must be candid that the conversation continues on the external service rather than pretending that messages are synchronized into Macro.

## Mission

Implement the smallest honest, usable maps-and-social-chat entry point consistent with issue #6039's two terse bullets: maps from physical calendar locations and recognizable X/Discord/map conversation or place links in Macro's existing shared content. Keep conversations on their source platform, with explicit external-opening language; do not invent an OAuth-backed inbox, read other people's private messages, or claim that a connector can send X/Discord DMs without provider permission. The existing Pipedream MCP catalog (`apps/web/src/features/settings/PipedreamIntegrations.tsx`, `apps/web/src/lib/core/pipedream/catalog.ts`) is **agent-tool access**, not native conversation sync; document that distinction. Issue #6039 has no further acceptance detail, so this is an explicit bounded interpretation of “chatting on x/ discord/ social medias,” not an assertion that an external messaging integration already exists. Preserve the ability to share and follow ordinary links to other social services rather than hard-coding an unsupported list as if it were exhaustive.
