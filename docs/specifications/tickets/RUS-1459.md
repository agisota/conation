## Mission

Build a durable corporate enrollment contract keyed by FusionAuth UUID (`macro_user.id`), normalized `macro|email` `User` profile, selected organization/team and owner-scoped mail principal. Only a verified administrator of the selected organization/team can issue, revoke, see and retry a generation-fenced exact-email invitation; domain verification is at most eligibility, not membership. Reconcile `user.create`, `user.create.complete`, `user.email.verified`, secondary verification, SSO/passwordless and concurrent logins while preserving self-serve; define a fail-closed fake-testable RUS-1461 handoff, store only RUS-1457 opaque references, and leave RUS-1458 as sole owner of signup marketing egress. Pending corporate grants never count as complete.
