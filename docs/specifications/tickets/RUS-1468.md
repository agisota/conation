## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

People can change or cancel one meeting in a repeating series without changing the others, including across daylight-saving changes. Guests can answer invitations and organizers see replies and cancellations after synchronization. Interrupted synchronization and delivery stay recoverable and visibly pending or failed instead of losing events, silently overwriting edits, or falsely claiming delivery.
