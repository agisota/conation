## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

People can see how much personal or team storage remains, and uploading to one does not consume the other's allowance. Concurrent uploads cannot overspend it, and failed uploads do not leave it stuck. Moving, replacing, deleting and restoring files keep totals accurate without exposing old or unconfirmed content.
