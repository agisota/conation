## Mission

Implement a provider-isolated JMAP producer and consumer for the RUS-1462 account/credential/remote-ID namespace on the *delivered, operator-selected* RUS-1450 durable queue. Poll every active account at least once per 15 seconds without push (and periodically catch up with push), exhaust initial Email/query and incremental Email/changes streams via bounded Email/get, persist projections and state with replay-safe transaction boundaries, and expose real resync jobs and truthful status through reached REST/UI readers.
