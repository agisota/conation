## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Participants can tell whether an internal meeting was actually recorded with permission and read a trustworthy account of what was said, or see an honest no-speech or failure state. Calendar-only and outside meetings can be saved without pretending a recording exists. Private meeting material is removed when permission is withdrawn or its retention period expires; old links are explicitly tested rather than assumed safe.
