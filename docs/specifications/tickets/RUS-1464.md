## What This Delivers

People can save drafts, send or forward messages with attachments, and organize company mail without going through Gmail. A retry after an uncertain response does not deliver another copy; the sender sees an honest pending, failed or confirmed result. Gmail keeps working; undo and scheduled sending are available only when cancellation really works.

## Mission

Deliver provider-selected JMAP server draft, MIME send/reply/forward and mailbox commands using *landed* RUS-1462 account/permission identities, RUS-1463 replay-safe sync, and RUS-1465 reusable authorized Ready blobs. Persist an actor/account-scoped idempotent send intent and distinguish pending, provider accepted/submitted, synced Sent, failed and uncertain. Never copy RUS-1465 storage/signed ACL/quota, use Gmail IDs for JMAP, conflate product From with RUS-1460 transactional mail, or claim RUS-1511 external SMTP/DNS delivery. The saved `plan` is design input, not the verbatim original request.
