## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Participants can review and correct proposed work from a genuinely recorded meeting, see where each proposal came from, and choose an eligible owner. Only their explicit approval turns the corrected proposal into one assigned task, even if delivery is repeated. Missing permission, doubtful evidence, ineligible owners, or a service outage leave a visible draft or error rather than a surprise task.
