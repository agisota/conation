## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Once basic Canvas saving is reliable, people can edit offline and reopen the same canvas with their edits in order and visibly pending, but only where approved protected storage is available. Older drafts move safely without appearing under another account, and downloading remains usable. When safe storage is unavailable, offline is visibly disabled rather than falsely promising saved or recoverable work. Shared editing and server-side sync are left for the next task.
