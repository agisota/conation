## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Self-host operators can keep their own separate secret settings and read-only access credentials for each service. Startup names missing essential settings without showing values, and unavailable or revoked access cannot silently use old settings. Operators get a safe rotation and exposed-credential response procedure, including an explicit choice about depending on an external provider.
