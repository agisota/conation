## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Only a call with its already saved recording permission can produce a video in the operator's private storage. An authorized participant can play and seek the video through a short-lived link; outsiders cannot obtain one. Missing permission or recording infrastructure shows a recording error, and repeated completion notifications never replace an accepted video.
