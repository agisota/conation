## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Resizable work panels sit flush together, separated by a fine line that remains easy to drag. Ordinary controls lose the heavy glass appearance while floating menus and dialogs retain theirs. At narrower widths, optional content or navigation yields instead of crushing open work; verified before-and-after evidence makes the result inspectable.
