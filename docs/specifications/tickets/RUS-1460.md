## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

A new employee can receive an invitation through a contact they can already access, prove they are its intended recipient and sign in before opening their new work mailbox. If that contact is lost, an authorized operator has an auditable, safe recovery option or a clear blocked state. Production never treats the local test inbox as external delivery; existing sign-ins continue to work.
