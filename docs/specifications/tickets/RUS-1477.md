## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Administrators can change only the brand they control, and every successful change records who made it and when. Unsafe logos cannot become public, visitors see only approved information, and simultaneous changes cannot silently overwrite one another. New images appear without a rebuild or stale cached image; previously published versions follow a documented retention policy.
