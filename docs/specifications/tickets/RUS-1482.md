## Goal and original user request

## What This Delivers

Operators can host calls on their own trusted addresses. Two people on different networks can hear and see one another, share a screen and resume after a connection interruption. If a relay is unavailable or the secure connection is untrusted, the call visibly fails with useful guidance instead of appearing connected. Operators have instructions to provision, rotate and check the service safely.

## Mission

Integrate a pinned operator-owned LiveKit service into the *landed* DEP-05 production self-host profile and SEC-01 secret contract, with CA-trusted WSS signaling, reachable publicly advertised ICE media and authenticated TURN/TLS. Use one operator-owned URL/key identity for document storage service's `LivekitRtcClient`, signed webhook and browser/iOS join token while retaining existing authorization, hosted and local-dev paths. T17 requires executable, production-path, distinct-network proof of audio/video, screen-share frames, recovery and working UDP-blocked relay; the same call surface must also visibly reject TURN-unavailable and invalid-TLS cases without claiming connected. No recording, STT or ACL implementation: CALL-02/03/04 own those.
